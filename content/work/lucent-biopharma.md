## problem

Visitors could not tell what stage the pipeline was at, or who the company was
talking to. Investors, clinical partners and prospective hires were all served
the same undifferentiated page.

## process

Five stakeholder interviews and a review of twelve comparable biopharma sites.
Three personas came out of it: investor, clinical partner, prospective hire.
Each was given a defined route through the site before any visual work started.

## design decisions

A 40-component design system in Figma, structured around the persona-led IA.
Pipeline stages modelled as CMS collections so the science team updates trial
data themselves. Typographic hierarchy carries the science; no stock imagery.

## handoff note

Figma component variants map one-to-one onto Webflow combo classes, so the built
site and the design file stay legible to each other. Two client seats migrated
with a written maintenance doc.
